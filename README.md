# Excel-Sales-Dashboard
Interactive Excel Sales Dashboard analyzing regional trends, product profitability, and monthly KPIs

---> Turning Raw Superstore Data into Actionable Sales Insights!I’m thrilled to share my latest data visualization project: an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard designed to empower stakeholders with rapid, data-driven decisions. 

By organizing high-level operational metrics alongside granular categorical data, this dashboard answers critical business questions at a single glance:

🔹 Core Performance: Tracked $484.25K in total revenue across 1,993 orders.
🔹 Seasonal Trends: Identified a significant Q4 revenue spike, peaking in September at $81.78K.
🔹 Regional Strengths: Highlighted the West Region as our top market leader ($147.88K).
🔹 Product Analytics: Revealed Technology as the highest-grossing category ($175.28K), heavily anchored by premium products like the Cisco TelePresence System ($22.64K).
